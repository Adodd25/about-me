## The Landscape

The cybersecurity market is in a consolidation-and-rebuild cycle. Platform vendors are acquiring fast, AI is reshaping every layer, and seed rounds are getting larger as founders race to own new categories before the platforms catch up.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total cyber VC financing, H1 2026 | ~$10.6B |
| AI-security seed rounds in 2026 | ~$855M across 150+ rounds |
| AI security share of Q2 2026 seed deals | ~25% |
| Q1 2026 cyber exit value | ~$32.9B (record, anchored by Wiz close) |
| Announced cyber M&A deals per year | ~400 pace |

## Reading List

Sources I follow to stay current on cybersecurity markets, threats, and investing.

### Core Sources

These are the six I actually subscribe to and read consistently.

| Source | Author | Cadence | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Return on Security](https://www.returnonsecurity.com/) | Mike Privette | Weekly | Security, Funded — weekly economic brief on cyber funding, M&A and category movement. The closest thing this industry has to a market tape. |
| [Strategy of Security](https://strategyofsecurity.com/) | Cole Grolmus | Ad hoc | Business and strategy of cyber. Tracks the billion-dollar cohort, the IPO pipeline and the real exit distribution. |
| [Venture in Security](https://ventureinsecurity.net/) | Ross Haleliuk | Weekly | Cyber startups, GTM, business models and VC. Wrote Cyber for Builders; runs an angel syndicate. |
| [Risky Business News](https://news.risky.biz/) | Patrick Gray, Catalin Cimpanu | Daily | The daily news floor, with actual editorial judgment about what matters. |
| [tl;dr sec](https://tldrsec.com/) | Clint Gibler | Weekly | The best weekly curation of appsec research, tools and talks. |
| [Embrace The Red](https://embracethered.com/blog/) | Johann Rehberger | Ad hoc | Practical attacks on AI agents and assistants. If you want to speak credibly about agent identity, read the attacks first. |
